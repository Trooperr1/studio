Pricing FAQs

Common questions about our pricing

❓

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees! All our pricing is transparent. The quoted price includes everything mentioned in the package. Any additional services will be discussed and approved by you first.

💳

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and cryptocurrency. Payment plans are available for enterprise packages.

🔄

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan at any time. We'll credit what you've already paid toward the new package.

⏰

What is the typical timeline?

Starter packages: 2-4 weeks. Professional: 4-8 weeks. Enterprise: 8-16 weeks. Timeline varies based on project complexity and your feedback speed.

🌐

Do you provide hosting?

We can recommend reliable hosting providers or set up hosting for you. Hosting costs are separate and typically range from $10-$100/month.

📞

What does support include?

Support includes bug fixes, minor updates, technical assistance, and consultation. Major feature additions may require additional fees.
